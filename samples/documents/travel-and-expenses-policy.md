# Travel and Expenses Policy — Northwind Labs (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic demo content written for KnowFlow. Northwind Labs is a fictional company.
> Policy owner: Finance. Policy ID FIN-TE-07. Last reviewed March 2026.

## Booking

All business travel is booked through the TravelDesk portal. Flights and trains must be booked
at least 14 days before departure unless the trip is urgent and the team lead confirms it in
writing. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than 6 hours; business class is allowed
for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Approvals

Trips with an estimated total cost up to 2,000 EUR are approved by the team lead. Trips above
2,000 EUR additionally need director approval. International trips outside the EU always need
director approval regardless of cost.

## Accommodation

Hotels are capped at 150 EUR per night in most cities. In London, Paris, Zurich and New York the
cap is 220 EUR per night. Stays above the cap need prior approval from Finance.

## Meals and per diem

The meal allowance is 40 EUR per travel day. For trips longer than 10 consecutive days the
allowance drops to 32 EUR per day from day 11 onwards. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Ground transport

Public transport is preferred. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for trips between 22:00 and
06:00, or when carrying equipment over 15 kg. Rental cars need team lead approval and must be the
smallest suitable category. Private car use is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses are submitted in the expense portal within 30 days of the end of the trip, with a
receipt for every item above 10 EUR. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected. Reimbursement
is paid with the next monthly salary after approval.
