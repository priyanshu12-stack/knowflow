# Employee Handbook 2026 — Northwind Labs (synthetic sample)

> Synthetic demo content written for KnowFlow. Northwind Labs is a fictional company.
> Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. Replaces the 2024 handbook.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees choose
when they work as long as they log 38 hours per week. Overtime must be approved in advance by the
team lead and is compensated as time off at a rate of 1.5 hours per overtime hour.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require a signed
remote-work agreement (form HR-RW-2) approved by the department head. Northwind Labs reimburses
up to 300 EUR once for home-office equipment, claimed through the expense portal within 90 days
of purchase.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 26 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days
may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March; carried-over days that are
not used by then expire. Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need approval
at least 30 days in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees must notify their team lead before 09:30 on the first day of illness. A medical
certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence. Sick days do not reduce
annual leave.

## Parental leave

Northwind Labs offers 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave to the primary caregiver and 8 weeks
of fully paid leave to the secondary caregiver. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the
birth or adoption. Employees must inform HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR for courses, conferences and books.
Unused budget does not carry over. Courses above 500 EUR need approval from the team lead before
booking.

## Equipment

New employees receive a laptop with full disk encryption enabled. Laptops are replaced every
four years, or earlier if repair costs exceed 40 percent of the replacement price. Lost or stolen
equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours via ticket category SEC-LOST.
