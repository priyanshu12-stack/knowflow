## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 10 unused days
may be carried over and must be used by 30 June of the following year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. There is no home-office equipment
allowance.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 800 EUR.
